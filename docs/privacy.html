Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Information We Collect

Bloom reads step count data from Apple Health to track your daily progress. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or shared with third parties.

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email, or location unless you voluntarily provide it.

How We Use Your Data

Step data is used solely to calculate your in-app progress, unlock restoration stages, and display statistics within the app.

Third-Party Services

We use Firebase (Google) for app functionality including daily events and push notifications. Firebase may collect anonymous usage analytics as described in Firebase's privacy policy.

Bloom+ subscriptions are processed by Apple; we use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history, as described in RevenueCat's privacy policy.

We use the Meta (Facebook) SDK only to count app installs and launches. It does not use your advertising identifier and does not track you across other apps.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, Bloom sends your weekly steps, best day, longest streak and achievement progress to Apple Game Center, so they appear in leaderboards and achievements. This data is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy together with your Game Center profile; we do not receive or store it on our servers. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in your device Settings.

Data Storage

Your progress, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage mechanisms.

Children's Privacy

Bloom is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@bloomapp.com
