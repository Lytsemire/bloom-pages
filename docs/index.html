Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How does step tracking work?
Bloom reads your steps from Apple Health. Make sure you've granted permission when the app first asked.

Q: What is Bloom+?
Bloom+ is our premium subscription that unlocks all restoration worlds, boosters, and exclusive content.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Bloom+ → Cancel.
